Privacy notice

This notice explains how Rengrow Consulting Crew (RCC) intends to handle information submitted through this website’s enquiry forms.

What the enquiry form collects

Your name, phone number, email address, chosen service, short message and consent to be contacted. The system also records the submission time, an enquiry reference and a request reference used to prevent duplicate submissions.

Why the information is used

To understand and respond to your requirement, arrange follow-up, track the enquiry’s progress and protect the form from duplicate or automated submissions. Authorised staff may add internal follow-up notes.

Where it is processed

The website is designed for static hosting on GitHub Pages. Form submissions pass through Google Apps Script into a private Google Sheet controlled by RCC. The administration interface uses Google-account authorization. Google and the website host may process technical information under their own policies.

Access and sharing

The spreadsheet must remain private. Access is intended for authorised RCC personnel and the service providers needed to operate the enquiry system. Any further sharing, including with a specialist professional, should be agreed where appropriate. Information may need to be disclosed where required by law.

Retention and your requests

RCC must set and document a retention period before launch. Enquiries and exported copies should be reviewed and removed when no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations. You may request a correction or deletion and withdraw permission for further enquiry follow-up. RCC may need to verify the request.

Contact RCC about your information: rengrowconsulting99@gmail.com or +91 82994 15370.

Browser storage and cookies

This website does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Form drafts and submission references are held in page memory, not localStorage. The protected dashboard uses Google’s sign-in system; Google may use cookies for authentication. Browser autofill is controlled by your browser. Language and theme preferences are saved in this browser using localStorage.

Keep sensitive information out of enquiries

Do not send PAN, Aadhaar, passwords, OTPs, bank credentials, payment card details or sensitive tax documents through these forms. They are for initial contact, not document exchange. If documents are needed, agree a suitable channel separately. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
